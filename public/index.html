How to Play

    Pick a name

    Type the name your friends will see on the leaderboard.

    Hosts create a room and share the code. Everyone else enters it to join the lobby.

  3. 3

    Answer fast

    Everyone sees the same question at once. You have 15 seconds — 10 points per correct answer.

    Take the trophy

    Watch the live leaderboard. The highest score after the final question wins!
